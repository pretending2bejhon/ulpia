## Glazes

Two options per order: **satin white** or **oatmeal**, both food-safe and dishwasher
tested. Rims are left unglazed as standard: glazed rims chip when cups are stacked wet.

## Lead time

Four weeks from confirmed order. Longer runs are split across deliveries rather than
delayed as one block.

## Terms

Payment on delivery. Monthly invoicing available for standing orders.
Every piece is thrown, trimmed, glazed and fired by hand in one studio, so batches vary
slightly in colour and that is the point.
