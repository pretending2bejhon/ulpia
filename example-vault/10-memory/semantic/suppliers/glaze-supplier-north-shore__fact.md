# North Shore Clay and Glaze

North Shore Clay and Glaze supplies the studio's clear, satin white and iron oxide slip.

## Units and pricing

North Shore sells glaze in 5 kg buckets with no order minimum beyond one bucket.
The clear runs cheapest of the three and the satin white carries a small surcharge for the
tin in it.

## Lead time

North Shore holds stock on the trade counter, so glaze is a same-week purchase and the
firing schedule does not have to plan around it.
