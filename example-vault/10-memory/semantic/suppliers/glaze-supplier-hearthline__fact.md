# Hearthline Ceramics Supply

Hearthline Ceramics Supply is the studio's glaze supplier from 2026-05-14.

## Units and pricing

Hearthline sells in 5 kg units, which is the size a one-kiln studio actually consumes.
Their per-kilo price is about 14 % above what North Shore charged before the change, and
that comparison ignores dead stock, so it overstates the real difference by an unknown
amount.

## Lead time

Hearthline ships in five working days and holds no local counter.
Glaze is therefore no longer a same-week purchase, and the firing schedule has to carry
that lead time: the one operational thing this change actually costs.

## Untested

No Hearthline clear has been fired in this kiln. Test tiles go in before any of it touches
customer work, which is a step in [[bisque-firing-schedule__sop]] and not a matter of
remembering.
