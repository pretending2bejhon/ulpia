# Tomas Ridley

Tomas Ridley repairs electric kilns across the county and has serviced the studio's kiln
since it was bought second-hand.

## What he covers

Tomas Ridley replaces elements, relay boards and thermocouples, and he will re-brick a
lid if the kiln is worth it.
He does not work on gas, and he will not sign off a kiln for a rented space he has not
seen.

## How he schedules

Tomas books by the week rather than the day and calls the morning he is coming.
A dead element in a working week is realistically three to five days of downtime, which is
the gap that turns a kiln fault into a missed delivery date.

## Why he is an entity and not a fact

Tomas Ridley is a stable subject that other claims point at, so he gets his own note rather
than a paragraph inside each of them. Anything bound to a date (the 2026-05-12 element
failure, what it cost, when he came) belongs in an episodic log, not here.
