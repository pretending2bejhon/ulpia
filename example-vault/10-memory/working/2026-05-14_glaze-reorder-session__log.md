# Glaze reorder session

One file for one session. Nothing here is durable yet — consolidation decides what is
promoted into `semantic/` and what is dropped.

## What happened

Mara needed clear and satin white before the Café Lumen glaze firing.
North Shore's order page now shows a 25 kg minimum per colour, up from 5 kg.
At 25 kg per colour the studio would be buying roughly two years of clear to fire one
order, and the satin white has a shelf life shorter than that.

Called Hearthline. They sell in 5 kg units, ship in five working days, and quote 14 % more
per kilo than North Shore's old price.

## Findings for consolidation

- [decision] Glaze supply moves to Hearthline from 2026-05-14.
- [constraint] Hearthline ships in five working days, so glaze is no longer a same-week
  purchase and the firing schedule has to absorb the lead time.
- [risk] Nobody has fired a Hearthline clear in this kiln. Test tiles before it touches a
  customer piece.
- [question] Is the 14 % higher unit price still cheaper once dead stock is counted? Not
  calculated this session.
